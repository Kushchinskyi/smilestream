Hello Jamendo licensing team,

We operate Smile Stream, an existing dental-video web application at https://smilestream.lovable.app. We are requesting a written licensing proposal, not purchasing or starting a subscription at this stage.

We need 60–120 distinct instrumental background tracks, preferably 90 (15 per style): refined hotel jazz/lounge, polished lo-fi/chillhop, warm non-dramatic ambient/piano, mellow deep house/chill electronic, refined acoustic/soft rock, and smooth modern instrumental pop/nu-disco. Each should preferably last 2–6 minutes. Please suggest a professionally coherent selection, not game music, cinematic tension, drones, aggressive drops or karaoke backing tracks.

Our application plays a continuous dental-video playlist, including advertising blocks, with a separate background-music channel. Music is not baked into a fixed audiovisual final cut and continues independently across video transitions. Users choose a music profile. We apply volume adjustment and ducking, without remixing the recordings.

MP3s must be stored on our own Supabase Storage/CDN and delivered to our existing HTML5 audio player on Samsung Tizen 2022 / Chromium 85. The current bucket is public; anyone with a file URL can access the unmodified MP3. The /tv route is also publicly accessible without login, including outside clinic premises. There is no music-download button, but we cannot describe delivery as technically access-restricted. We need to keep the existing player rather than replace it with your app or a proprietary player.

The initial use is one physical dental clinic in Ukraine with three TVs. We also need a contractual route for future independent clinics and their legal entities, not just branches of the first licensee. Please quote an initial pilot and separate expansion tiers for 10, 50 and 100 clinics, with per-location/device limits and rules for adding clients.

Please confirm explicitly:

1. Whether your In-Store partner/custom license can cover this complete use, including independent audio playback and clinical/advertising video context.
2. Download/supply of the selected original MP3 files, copying to our own server, public HTTPS/CDN delivery and the publicly accessible web player. Please identify any restrictions on raw URLs, caching or public access.
3. Rights in both the musical compositions and master recordings/performances, territories for online access and physical use in Ukraine, and whether clinics receive direct licenses or are covered by our platform agreement.
4. Whether Ukrainian statutory/equitable remuneration, collective-management fees and reporting remain payable. Identify who is responsible, which rights are covered by your payment, and provide supporting scope rather than a general “royalty-free” assurance.
5. Attribution, track/ISRC manifest, permitted duration, renewal/cancellation, required catalog updates and removal/retention rules after expiry or withdrawal of a track.
6. A written quote and sample agreement covering all of the above. Please do not activate a paid plan, charge us or enable automatic renewal.

Your current Terms of Sale make ordinary In-Store licenses non-sublicensable and restrict standalone use under Catalog licensing. We are therefore requesting an express platform agreement instead of assuming an ordinary subscription covers this use.

Thank you,
Smile Stream project
