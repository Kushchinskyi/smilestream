[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.en) covers the
affirmer's copyright and related rights, including copying, redistribution and public
performance, to the extent legally waivable, with a fallback licence. This supports
the proposed hosting/distribution model for rights the publisher holds; it does not
establish rights of unidentified third parties or eliminate statutory remuneration.

[2415-VIII](https://zakon.rada.gov.ua/laws/show/2415-19) require a separate assessment
of public-performance/phonogram remuneration and collective management. AI origin,
CC0, a free app, no direct music revenue and muting advertisements do not by themselves
prove an exemption. No conclusion that every clinic can use these recordings without
